The clock hums low against the wall,  
a tired pulse in a tired hall.  
Sleep keeps circling, never lands,  
static gathers in my hands.

Outside the window, streetlight hums,  
a steady beat where silence comes.  
I count the shadows, one by one,  
and wait for morning, wait for sun.
